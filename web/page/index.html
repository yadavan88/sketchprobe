Decode a serialized sketch in your browser

Paste an Apache DataSketches value from MongoDB, Parquet, Delta, Spark or Databricks and read what is inside. It runs on this page: nothing is uploaded.

  • HLL estimates how many distinct values there are.
  • Theta estimates distinct values and can be combined with other Theta sketches.
  • KLL estimates quantiles and percentiles of numbers, such as the median.
  • Quantiles is the classic quantiles sketch: ranks and percentiles for ordered values.
  • Frequency finds the most frequent items and their approximate counts.
